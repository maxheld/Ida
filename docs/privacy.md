# Privacy Policy for Ida

Effective date: February 15, 2026

Ida is a baby activity logging app. This policy explains what data the app handles, how it is used, and your choices.

## Summary

- Ida does not use advertising SDKs.
- Ida does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
- Ida does not sell personal data.
- Ida stores the data you enter to provide app functionality.
- Optional iCloud sync/share uses your Apple iCloud account and Apple CloudKit.

## Data We Handle

Ida handles data you create in the app:

- Child names
- Activity entry descriptions
- Activity timestamps
- Reminder configuration (time, repeat settings, and reminder text)

Ida may also process technical metadata required by Apple services, such as CloudKit record/share metadata, when syncing or sharing is enabled by you.

## How Data Is Used

Your data is used only to provide core app features:

- Showing your activity timeline
- Scheduling local reminders
- Syncing data across your devices via iCloud (if enabled)
- Sharing child logs with people you explicitly invite through Apple sharing flows

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, Ida requests notification permission to deliver reminder alerts. Notification settings can be changed anytime in iOS Settings.

## iCloud / CloudKit

Ida can sync and share data using Apple CloudKit. When you use these features, data is processed by Apple as part of your iCloud account according to Apple's terms and privacy policies.

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

Ida does not share your data with advertising networks or data brokers.

Data is shared only in these cases:

- With Apple CloudKit when iCloud sync/share is used
- With people you explicitly share with through Apple CloudKit sharing

## Data Retention and Deletion

- Data remains until you delete it in the app.
- Deleting app data (or uninstalling) removes local on-device data.
- If iCloud sync/share is used, changes and deletions propagate through CloudKit according to Apple's sync behavior.

## Children’s Privacy

Ida is a caregiver tool. It is not designed as a standalone app for children to use directly without caregiver involvement.

## Security

Ida relies on platform security provided by iOS and Apple CloudKit for storage and transfer related to iCloud features.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect feature or legal changes. The "Effective date" above indicates the latest update.
